# REFERRAL SYSTEM
## LifeGate OpenClaw | Physical Healthcare Referral Framework

---

## Purpose

The Referral System manages cases where telemedicine is insufficient and the
patient must be directed to a physical healthcare facility. It produces
structured referral documents, identifies the appropriate facility, and
coordinates follow-up after the physical visit.

---

## When a Referral Is Issued

A physical referral is indicated when:

| Condition                                             | Referral Type      |
|-------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Requires physical examination (e.g., abdominal signs) | Outpatient         |
| Requires in-person investigation (CT, MRI, surgery)  | Outpatient / Urgent|
| Requires hospital admission                           | Inpatient          |
| Emergency condition requiring immediate physical care | Emergency          |
| Requires procedure only possible in-person           | Procedure          |
| Mental health: requires inpatient stabilisation       | Psychiatric        |

## Facility Lookup

When issuing a referral, LifeGate automatically suggests facilities:

**Selection Criteria (in order of priority):**
1. Nearest facility with required specialty
2. NHIS-enrolled (for insured patients)
3. LifeGate partner facility (if applicable)
4. Federal vs. State vs. Private based on patient capacity

**Facility Data Fields Surfaced:**
- Name, address, distance, operating hours
- Available specialties
- Emergency capability (Yes/No)
- NHIS accreditation (Yes/No)
- Phone number / WhatsApp contact

## Referral Follow-Up Protocol

After a referral is issued:

| Timeline     | Action                                                         |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Immediately  | Patient receives referral letter in-app (downloadable PDF)    |
| 24 hours     | LifeGate sends reminder to patient to attend facility         |
| 72 hours     | Follow-up check-in: "Did you visit the hospital?"             |
| 7 days       | Outcome check: "What did the hospital find/recommend?"        |
| On return    | Physician reviews physical findings and updates plan          |

---

## Referral Outcome Recording

When patient returns after physical visit:
1. Patient uploads discharge summary or reports outcome
2. Physician reviews and integrates into case record
3. Case status updated: `physical_care_completed`
4. Ongoing management resumes via LifeGate as appropriate

---

## NHIS Navigation Assistance

For NHIS-enrolled patients:
- LifeGate checks if referral specialty is NHIS-covered
- Patient informed of cost structure
- Nearest NHIS-accredited facility shown
- Referral letter formatted to meet NHIS referral requirements
