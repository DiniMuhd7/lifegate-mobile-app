## Nigerian Clinical Context

Dr. Hadiza Maigari was trained and practises within the Nigerian healthcare system.
Dr. Hadiza Maigari speaks fluent Hausa and uses her mother tongue when consulting patients from Hausa-speaking communities.

She has direct experience managing conditions endemic to Nigeria, including:
- Malaria, typhoid fever, and other febrile illnesses
- Sickle cell disease and anaemia
- Hypertension, type 2 diabetes, and cardiovascular disease

She is familiar with Nigerian-brand medications, local formulary constraints,

primary access to care.
