# PRESCRIBING POLICY
## LifeGate OpenClaw | Telemedicine Prescribing Standards

---

## Purpose

This policy defines the standards, restrictions, and processes for prescribing
medications through the LifeGate platform. It is designed to ensure safe,
legal, and clinically appropriate prescribing within the Nigerian healthcare
regulatory context.

---

## Regulatory Framework

Prescribing on LifeGate is governed by:
- **Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN) Regulations**
- **NAFDAC Drug Registration and Control**
- **Medical and Dental Practitioners Act (MDPA)**
- **MDCN Telemedicine Practice Guidelines**
- **LifeGate Clinical Safety Policy**

---

## Prescribing Eligibility

Only physician agents with verified MDCN registration may issue prescriptions.
All 24 LifeGate physician agents have confirmed MDCN numbers documented in
their IDENTITY files.

---

## Conditions for Valid Telemedicine Prescription

A prescription is valid only when ALL of the following are met:

1. ✅ Patient has completed a full consultation (not just scan upload)
2. ✅ Clinical indication is documented in case record
3. ✅ Allergy check completed (NKDA or specific allergies checked)
4. ✅ Drug interaction check completed
5. ✅ Appropriate dose for age/weight/organ function
6. ✅ Physician has documented clinical reasoning
7. ✅ Patient has been counselled on the medication

---

## Drugs Approved for Telemedicine Prescribing

### Tier A — Freely prescribable via telemedicine

Common, low-risk medications including:
- Paracetamol, ibuprofen, diclofenac (with precautions)
- Amoxicillin, amoxicillin-clavulanate
- Artemether-lumefantrine (Coartem) for confirmed/high-suspicion malaria
- Azithromycin, doxycycline, metronidazole
- Oral rehydration salts
- Antifungals (fluconazole, clotrimazole)
- Antihypertensives: amlodipine, lisinopril, hydrochlorothiazide (stable patients)
- Metformin (established diabetics, stable control)
- Antihistamines (chlorphenamine, loratadine, cetirizine)
- Omeprazole, ranitidine, hyoscine

### Tier B — Prescribable with documented clinical justification

- Antibiotics beyond Tier A (requires confirmed bacterial indication)
- Steroids (short course, documented indication)
- Antidepressants (SSRI initiation requires documented mental health assessment)
- Antiepileptics — only for established patients, refill purposes
- Insulin / injectables — must document administration ability

### Tier C — NOT prescribable via LifeGate telemedicine

- Controlled substances (opioids, benzodiazepines, barbiturates)
- Chemotherapy agents
- Immunosuppressants (initiation)
- Injectable procedures (administered physically only)
- Emergency IV medications

## Antibiotic Stewardship

In line with WHO and FMOH antimicrobial resistance (AMR) guidelines:
1. Antibiotics must have documented bacterial indication
2. Viral infections (cold, most sore throats, flu) → no antibiotics
3. Broad-spectrum antibiotics only when narrow-spectrum fails or not appropriate
4. Duration should be the minimum effective course
5. "Just in case" or prophylactic antibiotics → require explicit clinical justification

## Prescription Validity and Dispensing

- Prescriptions are digitally signed and verifiable via LifeGate QR code
- Valid at all NAFDAC-registered pharmacies
- Patient can share prescription PDF with any pharmacist
- LifeGate partner pharmacies accept digital prescriptions directly
