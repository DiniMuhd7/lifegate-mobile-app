What This Is

Clinically grounded.
Anonymised by design.

LifeGate's EDIS engine and physician review network generate a continuous stream of validated health signals across Nigeria. This dashboard surfaces those signals — aggregated, de-identified, and structured — as a public good.

Physician-Validated

Every data point is backed by a licensed, MDCN-verified physician. Not raw AI output. Not self-reports.

Privacy-First

All data is anonymised and aggregated under NDPA 2023. No personal or patient-identifiable information is ever exposed.

Real-Time

Signals update continuously from the live LifeGate platform — not periodic batch reports collected weeks after the fact.

Geography-Aware

Condition signals are mapped to state and LGA level — enabling regional health planning and outbreak geo-fencing.
